KGOT: Unified Knowledge Graph & Optimal Transport Pseudo-Labeling for Molecule–Protein Interaction Prediction

Turning unlabeled molecule–protein pairs into supervision through OT, over a unified biomedical knowledge graph

Jiayu Qin · Zhengquan Luo · Guy Tadmor · Changyou Chen · David Zeevi · Zhiqiang Xu  —  University at Buffalo · MBZUAI · Weizmann Institute of Science

Motivation

Molecule–protein interaction (MPI) prediction drives drug discovery and function annotation, yet two problems persist:

  • ① Label scarcity — known pairs cover a tiny slice of real interactions.
  • ② Ignored context — feature-only models discard genes, pathways and functional annotations that signal interaction.

KG supplies the missing biological context; OT converts unlabeled pairs into consistent pseudo-labels.

A · Unified knowledge graph

Six databases fused into one heterogeneous graph:

ChEBI + UniProtKEGGGene Ontology PFamENZYMEDrugBank
  • 8 node types (molecules, proteins, genes, GO terms, pathways, families, EC…)
  • ~29 relation types over millions of edges
  • Nodes carry pretrained molecule / protein structural features

Pipeline

A · Integrate data6 sources → unified KG
▼
B · Score functiontrain S(x,y) via inverse OT
▼
C · Pseudo-labelsSinkhorn OT + similarity constraint on unlabeled pairs
▼
D · Augment & predictadd pseudo-interaction edges → KG embeddings

B · Score via inverse optimal transport

Fuse molecule & protein embeddings with an MLP:

$$S(x,y)=W\big(f(x)\oplus g(y)\big),\ \ W:[512,256,1]$$

Align the predicted transport plan (cost $C_{pred}=1-S$) to the ground-truth plan of real interactions:

$$\mathcal{L}_{score}=\mathrm{KL}\!\left(T_{pred}\,\|\,T_{gt}\right)$$

C · OT pseudo-labeling

For unlabeled pairs, solve entropic OT with a similarity constraint:

$$\min_{T\in\Pi(r,c)}\ \langle T,C\rangle-\varepsilon H(T)+\lambda\,\Omega_{sim}(T)$$
  • Sinkhorn–Knopp solves it efficiently
  • Similarity constraint keeps similar molecules’ labels consistent
  • Scores above threshold $\delta$ → high-confidence pseudo-interactions

D · Multi-objective link prediction

Inject $T$ as a new pseudo-interaction relation; train KG embeddings with a joint loss:

$$\mathcal{L}_{total}=\mathcal{L}_{KG}+\alpha\,\mathcal{L}_{pseudo}$$

Model-agnostic — works with PairRE, RotatE, MuRE, TorusE, ComplEx-FF. “Filling in” likely interactions yields a more complete graph.

Conclusion

  • Unifying biological context + OT pseudo-labeling gives label-efficient MPI prediction.
  • New SOTA on DUD-E & LIT-PCBA; consistent link-prediction gains.
  • Model-agnostic, scalable, and effective when labels are scarce.
